# Research writing rules

The introduction follows hook, theoretical relationship, literature gap, contributions and research questions.

Methodological triangulation must match the research question and data structure. Each evidence layer has an explicit role, estimand and dependence relationship with the others. Complementary methods do not automatically supply independent confirmation or causal identification. For this study: independently dated events and sampled news establish context; paired market observations measure relative repricing; contract verification, local-rate conditioning and instrument-yield reconciliation test its interpretation. Disagreement across layers is reported rather than resolved through significance-based selection.

Baseline and controlled models are compared on identical observations. Direct contrasts test differences between coefficients; different significance labels are insufficient. Report the full planned or retrospective sensitivity grid and its status. Country mechanisms require identification beyond one instrument pair per country.

APA 7 references, table and figure citations, Times New Roman text and right-aligned equation numbers remain the document conventions.
